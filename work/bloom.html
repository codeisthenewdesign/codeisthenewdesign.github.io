Brand Identity

AI 시대의 불안인 AI Blue를 지나 저마다의 방식으로 피어난다(Bloom)는 커뮤니티의 모티브를 웃는 꽃 캐릭터로 만들었습니다. 글자 로고에서도 O 하나를 분홍 꽃으로 피워 같은 이야기를 담았습니다.

Color

From a stiff AI meetup
to a community anyone wants to join

Overview

Bloom is Korea’s largest AI community, where developers, founders, marketers, and researchers share how they work with AI. To turn stiff AI meetups into a community anyone wants to join, I handled its brand, character, event thumbnails, website, and YouTube.

Branding

Turning stiff AI meetups into a community anyone wants to join

YouTube

Keeping talks that vanished overnight as a bridge to the meetup

A community
anyone wants to join

Brand Identity

The community’s motif is moving past AI Blue, the anxiety of the AI era, to bloom in your own way. I turned it into a smiling flower character. In the wordmark, one O blooms into a pink flower to tell the same story.

Color

Bloom Pink, taken from the character, is the main color and softens the stiff AI meetup image. Dark Bloom Ink sits behind it so the pink and the event thumbnails catch the eye first.

  • #FFB3C7Bloom PinkRGB 255, 179, 199
  • #0A0A0ABloom InkRGB 10, 10, 10
  • #FDFDFDBloom PaperRGB 253, 253, 253

Event Thumbnail

To stand apart from other AI meetups, I used the main character to make images tied to each event’s topic.

Website

I built the website to show what events Bloom has run and what kind of community it is.

Visit website

YouTube

Great talks vanished that night and people who couldn’t come had no way to hear them, so I kept the talks on video. Originals like founder interviews, the i-Podcast, and a series visiting AI-native companies keep Bloom in view between meetups.

Visit channel

Result

Across 40+ events, Bloom reached 7,497 unique applicants, a 52% return rate, and 4,250 YouTube subscribers.
